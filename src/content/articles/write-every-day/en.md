title: "Write every day"
listTitle: "Why write every day?"
---

It doesn’t matter whether you have a Telegram channel or work as an editor — you have to write. Every day.

When I had just started my channel, I wrote every day for 3–4 months without a break. Sometimes several posts a day. I still write every day: posts, scripts, work documents. What has changed over that time?

First, structure in my speech and thoughts became automatic. Imagine your brain giving answers as clear, logical and structured as a neural network does. Or as a text prepared in advance?

It becomes reality. At first you spend a long time coming up with a structure and rewrite it many times. Then you know in advance how a topic can be unpacked. After that it happens automatically inside your head, and you just write down the words after your inner narrator.

Second, you chicken out less. Sharing something, telling a story, drawing people’s attention to yourself when you need to. You get used to being open. And you stop being embarrassed about your thoughts, interests and reasoning. Because even those found their reader.

Over 18 months there we’ve hated and loved pets, discussed guys named Anton and childhood memories, joked about my OnlyFans, and lived through fuck-ups at work.

Third, you get more complex. After 30–40 identical posts of “headline + 2 paragraphs of arguments”, a new stage begins. How do I tell the story in a more interesting way? How do I infuriate people? Make them fall in love? How do I build the drama?

And, again, you start speaking better. Persuading, arguing, listening, asking questions. Everyone has ambitions. And I think for many they are tied to influence — and influence goes to orators.

So, write every day.
