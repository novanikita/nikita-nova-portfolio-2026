---
title: "“Street Wave”, a book"
---

Brief: lay out a documentary research book about street art in Saint Petersburg.

The book gives the basics of street art to people who know nothing about it but would like to.

The first part collects 13 articles by different experts. They look at street art from different angles: what it means, why and how it appeared, and what several key Russian artists think about it.

The second and third parts are my own research of Saint Petersburg. I chose 5 city objects of different scale and purpose, from a pedestrian crossing to a whole apartment building, scanned them with LiDAR and documented every piece of street art on them. I took about 5,000 photos for the book and selected and edited 3,000 of them for its pages.

The cover a buyer gets can show any letter of the Russian or Latin alphabet, or a digit.

Made in 3 months in 2022
